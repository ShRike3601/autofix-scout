# What is new in AutoFix Scout 0.3.0

- Performance check: AutoFix Scout looks for reasons why the computer has become slow, such as the processor slowing down because of heat or power settings, the power plan, an old BIOS and memory that runs slower than its rating.
- Graphics check: AutoFix Scout reads the state of the graphics card, its driver and its temperature, and points out settings that often reduce speed in games.
- Stress test: an optional test of about one minute that puts the processor and the graphics card under load while their readings are watched. It stops by itself if the computer becomes too hot.
- Wi-Fi watcher: an optional helper that reconnects to a saved Wi-Fi network when the connection drops. It can be turned off at any time.
- One-click updates: when a newer version is available, AutoFix Scout offers to install it. Nothing is installed until you select "Update", and Windows asks for your permission once.
- History with undo: every change AutoFix Scout made is listed with its date. Where an undo is possible, you can put the change back.
